# Market notes — Indian personal loans (illustrative)

Retail unsecured loans are priced off bureau score, FOIR, employer category and existing leverage.
A ₹8 lakh / 4-year request with score 720 is in-market for large private banks if FOIR stays near or below 50%.
Processing fees commonly 1–2%. Rates often 10.5%–16% for good-score salaried borrowers.
