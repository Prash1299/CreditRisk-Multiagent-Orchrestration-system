# Unsecured Personal Loan Policy (illustrative)

Applies to salaried personal-loan requests in INR.

- Minimum bureau score: 680 (prefer 700+)
- Maximum FOIR including proposed EMI: 50% (refer 50–55%, not suitable above 55%)
- Maximum tenure: 60–72 months depending on lender
- Amount should sit inside product min/max band
- Existing EMI already high relative to income is a key risk driver
- This policy supports **decision support** only. A human underwriter must confirm KYC, income proofs and bureau details.

## Pricing sketch

- Score 750+: lower end of lender rate range
- Score 700–749: mid range
- Score below 680: generally not suitable for unsecured personal loans
